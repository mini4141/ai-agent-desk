Gear care and warranty

How long is the warranty?

Tents and packs carry a 5-year warranty against manufacturing defects.

How do I wash my down jacket?

Wash gently in a front-loading machine with down-specific soap and tumble dry low with dryer balls.

Can you repair my backpack?

We repair zippers and straps for free within the warranty and for a small fee afterwards.

How do I waterproof my tent?

Clean the fly, let it dry fully and apply a spray-on waterproofing treatment every season.

Does the warranty cover normal wear?

No. Normal wear and accidental damage are not covered, but we offer repairs at cost.

How do I make a warranty claim?

Send us your order number and photos of the problem and we will approve a replacement or repair.
