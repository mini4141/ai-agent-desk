Account and support

How do I reset my password?

Use Forgot password on the sign-in page and follow the emailed link.

What are your support hours?

Our team is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm Pacific. The AI assistant is available 24/7.

Can I talk to a person?

Yes. Ask the chat for a human and a teammate will join during support hours.

Can I book a callback?

Share your phone number and a preferred time and we will call you back.

Do you have retail stores?

We have stores in Seattle, Portland and Denver, open 10am to 8pm daily.
