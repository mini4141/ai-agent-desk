Orders and payment

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay.

Can I cancel my order?

Orders can be cancelled until they ship. After that, use the return process.

When is my card charged?

Your card is charged when the order ships, not when you place it.

Do you offer gift cards?

Digital gift cards from $25 to $500 are delivered by email instantly.

How do promo codes work?

Enter one code at checkout. Codes cannot be combined with other offers.

Can I get an invoice?

Invoices are available as PDFs in Order History.
