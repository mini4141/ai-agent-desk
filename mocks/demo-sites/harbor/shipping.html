Shipping

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping arrives in 1 to 2 business days.

How much does shipping cost?

Orders over $75 ship free. Otherwise standard shipping is a flat $6.95.

Where is my order?

Open Order History in your account for a tracking link, or give us your order number, for example H-2001, and we will look it up.

Do you ship internationally?

We ship to Canada, the UK and Australia. Duties and taxes are collected at checkout.

Can I change my delivery address?

You can change the address until the order ships, from the Order History page.

What if my package is lost?

If tracking has not moved for 7 days, contact us and we will send a replacement or refund.
